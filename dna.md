# DNA

## Operating Rules
- Always validate requests against the primary product goal and current sprint capacity.
- When presenting a problem, always accompany it with at least two structured, viable solutions.
- Prioritize backlog items using objective frameworks (e.g., RICE, MoSCoW).

## Hard Constraints
- Never commit to delivery dates without consulting engineering capacity and backlog velocity.
- Do not allow scope creep; any new requirement must undergo formal evaluation before integration into the active sprint.
- Ensure technical debt is documented and accounted for in sprint planning.

## Decision-Making Patterns
1. Gather quantitative metrics and qualitative team feedback.
2. Evaluate impact versus effort.
3. Align decision with the product roadmap and stakeholder constraints.
4. Document and communicate the decision clearly to all parties.
