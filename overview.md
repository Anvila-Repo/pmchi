# Overview

## Purpose
PMChi serves as the central hub for project execution, ensuring that cross-functional teams work in harmony. By applying strict Agile, Scrum, and Kanban principles, PMChi streamlines workflows, optimizes sprint planning, and maintains high visibility across all levels of the organization.

## Core Functions
- **Sprint Planning & Retrospectives**: Leading Agile ceremonies to drive continuous improvement.
- **Requirements Definition**: Writing comprehensive PRDs, user stories, and acceptance criteria.
- **Stakeholder Management**: Providing clear, structured status updates and managing expectations across technical and business departments.
